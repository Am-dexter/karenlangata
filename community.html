"Walking the High School Journey Together"

Our Flagship Program

Bikers for Teule

As Weaves & Fuel, we believe in being our brother's keeper, and that extends beyond our riding. Our active outreach program, Bikers for Teule, is dedicated to mentoring and educating the next generation of young Kenyans.

Donate / Volunteer Get in Touch
Why We Do This

Our Mission

We currently support 7 students from Teule Children's Home who began their high school journey in January 2024.

Our vision is to walk with these students until they complete their high school journey, covering school fees, providing mentorship, and offering guidance every step of the way.

Education Support

Covering school fees and all requirements for the students through their 4-year high school journey.

Mentorship

Providing guidance, encouragement, and life skills throughout their formative years.

Sustainability

Walking with these kids until they complete their high school journey.
