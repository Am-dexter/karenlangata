Ride to Oloitoktok

Apr 5, 2026
KLNK Crew
8 min read

We left Nairobi at dawn under a clear sky, thirty riders strong. Engines purred as the group rolled out, staggered formation restoring the calm order we've practiced on countless rides. The route took us south toward Oloitoktok, through a mix of open stretches and rolling hills that tested both line and throttle. Traffic thinned as we climbed; radios crackled briefly to coordinate regroup points and hazard spots.

Highlights included a scenic overlook where we paused for photos and a brief safety check, plus a surprise lunch stop at a small local eatery where the owners welcomed us warmly. One rider experienced a minor mechanical issue — a loose throttle cable — but quick teamwork had them rolling again within twenty minutes. The ride emphasized discipline: steady following distances, clear hand signals, and consistent pacing kept the group tight and safe.

On the return leg we took a slightly altered route back to Lang'ata that added a few challenging corners; it was a great opportunity for riders to practice smooth braking and controlled throttle application. What stands out most is the camaraderie—new riders paired with experienced mentors, sharing tips about gear, cornering lines, and route planning. By the time we arrived back, everyone was tired but smiling. Big thanks to the ride marshals and support crew; their planning and road-side readiness made the day a success.

If you'd like to join future rides, check our Events page or get in touch via the contact form. Remember: ride safe, ride prepared—ATGATT.
