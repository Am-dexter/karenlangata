The Executive Bench

Driven by
Vision, Anchored in Discipline

Behind the seamless orchestration of #TheBestest NK is a structured governance framework bound by the KLNK Bylaws and Code of Conduct. Backed by the community and structured for the 2026/27 calendar, this leadership structure locks down strategic vision, operational excellence, and absolute accountability in every single run.

Leadership & Governance

Committee Structure

Chairperson / Executive Director

The principal leader responsible for strategic direction, executive coordination, and the club’s long-term governance.

Vice Chairperson / Deputy Executive Director

Supports leadership execution, safeguards continuity, and steps in to maintain operational rhythm when the chairperson is off-road.

Treasurer / Chief Financial Officer

Maintains the club’s financial discipline, manages dues, and reports transparently on funding needs and expenditures.

Member Welfare Officer & Chief Whip

Looks after member wellbeing, enforces internal accountability, and ensures every rider stays aligned with KLNK standards.

Training & Safety Officer

Champions ride safety, training programs, and ATGATT compliance to protect members during every single group ride.

Organizing Secretary

Coordinates meetings, manages logistics, and keeps the club’s calendar and official communication flowing clearly.

Impact & Outreach Coordinator

Leads external partnerships, outreach programs, and the club’s shared commitments to the broader community.

Assistant Impact & Outreach Coordinator

Assists external partnerships, outreach programs, and the club’s shared commitments to the broader community.
