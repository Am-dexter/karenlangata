ATGATT — Why Full Gear Is Non-Negotiable

Mar 20, 2026
KLNK Crew
6 min read

ATGATT stands for All The Gear, All The Time. It's not just a slogan we throw around—it's a fundamental principle that every NK member commits to on the road. Wearing full protective gear is more than a rule; it's a mindset and a commitment to coming home safe every time you ride.

The reality is sobering: when you're on two wheels, there's no airbag, no crumple zone. In Nairobi's traffic—where roads are unpredictable, surfaces vary, and hazards appear without warning—the only protection between you and serious injury is what you're wearing. A certified helmet protects your brain. A quality motorcycle jacket distributes impact and resists abrasion. Gloves save your hands and wrists. Reinforced pants protect your legs. And proper boots keep your feet and ankles stable.

We've asked long-time NK riders why they never skip a piece of kit, and the answers are consistent: practice. Yes, practice. Low-speed slides at parking lots, gravel encounters on country roads, sudden stops in traffic—these scenarios teach you what gear actually does. When you've felt pavement through your jacket, you never question ATGATT again. When you've caught your boot on a curb and felt the protection work, it clicks. This isn't theory; it's lived experience.

The cost argument? Yes, quality gear costs money. A good helmet runs Ksh 15,000–40,000. A reinforced jacket might be Ksh 10,000–25,000. But a hospital bill for road rash removal, skin grafts, or worse—that's exponentially more. One of our members skipped gloves once. The resulting palm scarring took three years to fade. Another rode without a proper jacket and ended up with a fractured collarbone and road rash that left permanent scars. Both now preach ATGATT like their lives depend on it—because, frankly, they do.

For members on a budget, prioritize in this order: certified helmet (non-negotiable), gloves, textile jacket, reinforced pants, proper boots. You can upgrade over time, but start with these five. Borrow from another rider, rent, save up—but never compromise on the foundation. And remember: ATGATT applies to every ride, whether it's a 2km trip to the market or a 200km weekend trip. That's when accidents happen: on the "quick runs," when riders get complacent.

The KLNK community takes this seriously. We've created a used-gear swap channel where members can find affordable options. We've negotiated discounts with local gear shops. We welcome riders who are building their gear collection. But we never compromise on the principle: if you're on a bike, you're fully geared. Period.

Ride safe. Ride prepared. ATGATT, always.
