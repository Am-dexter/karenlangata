    Join the NK

    This is more than a riding group. It's a brotherhood, a sisterhood, a community. Here's what it takes to be part of The Bestest NK.

    Requirements

    Eligibility Criteria

    To join "The Bestest NK," prospective members must meet the following criteria:

    Private Biker Status

    You must be a Private Biker , an individual who owns or regularly rides a motorcycle for personal use, commuting, or recreation. Commercial use (courier/boda boda) does not qualify.

    Motorcycle Ownership

    You must own a motorcycle or provide proof of regular riding access.

    Valid Class A2 Riding License

    A valid Class A2 riding license is required for all members. No exceptions.

    Location , Karen or Lang'ata Area

    You must live or work within Karen, Lang'ata, Nairobi West, South C, or provide a compelling reason for joining this specific NK.

    Insurance Coverage

    Valid motorcycle insurance AND personal medical insurance (SHIF or private cover) must be maintained at all times.

    ATGATT Commitment

    You must commit to the All The Gear All The Time (ATGATT) safety standard , full riding gear for every official ride. Always.

    Active Participation

    Attend at least one NK activity (ride, meeting, or training) before formal admission and maintain active engagement thereafter.

    KLNK Code of Conduct

    Agree to comply with the KLNK Bylaws and Code of Conduct, which emphasize discipline, accountability, and respect for all.

    How to Join

    The 3-Step Process

    1
    Apply

    Fill out the NK Engagement & ICE (In Case of Emergency) forms. This is your first step to becoming part of The Bestest NK.

    2
    Probation

    Join the "Weaves & Fuel Lounge" for 3 months. Learn the culture, meet the crew, and experience what NK is all about.

    3
    Ride

    Participate in at least one official NK activity , a ride, meeting, or training session , for formal admission into The Bestest NK.

    The Weaves & Fuel Lounge

    The Lounge is our probationary community space , a 3-month immersive period where prospective members get to know the community, understand our values, attend rides, and demonstrate they embody the Bestest NK spirit before being formally inducted.
