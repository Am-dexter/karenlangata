Our Identity

Karen Langata
Nyumba Kumi

Founded in 2015 as the Karen Langata Ngong Rongai Nyumba Kumi, we've grown into one of Nairobi's most active and respected riding communities. In 2018, leadership expanded into three specialized units , and KLNK has never looked back.

Covering Lang'ata Road, Dagoretti Road, Nairobi West, South C, and Karen , we live by one creed: "The Bestest NK."

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Safety First

Strict compliance with traffic laws, ride protocols, and ATGATT , All The Gear, All The Time, every single ride.

Excellence

Embracing the "Bestest NK" mindset , showing up, contributing, and elevating the culture of motorcycle riding.

Respect & Honor

Maintaining integrity toward fellow members, other riders, and the public , on and off the road.

Inclusivity

Welcoming riders of all experience levels, genders, and styles , if you ride, you belong.

Community Support

Fostering brotherhood and sisterhood through mentorship, welfare, and giving back beyond the road.

Governance

Operating under formal PBAK-aligned bylaws ensuring discipline, accountability, and safety in all activities.
